NEGATIVE FINDING. On all 4 strict-external runs, the high-confidence/low-ERS group shows LOWER classification error than the high-confidence/high-ERS group (median external Fisher OR = 0.153), while its held-out explanation stability S_challenge remains lower (0.73-0.86 vs 0.81-0.93). In at least one external setting the relationship survives conditioning on class. Therefore: high confidence + low ERS does NOT reliably imply high classification risk. Explanation reliability and prediction correctness are distinct properties; a stable explanation can accompany an incorrect prediction (and vice versa).
